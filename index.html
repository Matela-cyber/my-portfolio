A little about me

Building for people,
not just screens.

I’m a Software Engineering with Multimedia graduate who builds useful web, mobile and desktop applications, bringing engineering foundations together with usability and visual clarity.

My strongest practical work is building full-stack web applications, Firebase-backed platforms, mobile experiences and desktop software. Projects such as PTROS, TechHub Lesotho and LAQS show how I apply those skills to real product needs.
